**AI-Native User Flow · Juno**

**Entry Point**

Signal type: New customer message / transcript received

A new P0 customer transcript is uploaded to the Raw Input column in Juno.

What the PM sees instantly

A status pill appears beneath the transcript:

"Juno is analyzing customer evidence…"

The Insights and PRD columns dim to ~40% to indicate that Juno is working.

Juno operates as a Hybrid (RAG + Agentic) Copilot: it retrieves evidence, evaluates strategic alignment, recommends priorities, and drafts rationale. The PM remains responsible for reviewing and approving the recommendation.

**The Flow **
Analyze customer signal
Juno identifies the primary customer pain point and supporting evidence from the transcript.

Retrieve strategic evidence
RAG searches the RocketShip Strategy One-Pager and retrieves the top relevant strategic clauses (top-K = 6).

Cross-reference evidence
Agentic comparison logic evaluates whether the customer pain point aligns with one or more strategic pillars.

Develop recommendation
Juno evaluates customer severity, frequency, strategic alignment, and available evidence to recommend P0–P3 or notRecommended.

Generate defensible rationale
Juno explains why the item received its recommended priority and cites the customer and strategy evidence supporting the recommendation.

Provide an alternative
When appropriate, Juno proposes a defensible alternative to the original request that may deliver the desired customer or business outcome faster or with greater impact.

Draft PRD content
Juno converts the validated insight, evidence, recommendation, and rationale into a draft PRD section.

PM review and approval
The PM reviews the recommendation, evidence, rationale, and PRD draft. The PM can approve, edit, override, or reject Juno's recommendation before anything is written to the roadmap.

**Progress States**

Juno communicates what it is doing rather than appearing to work silently:

"Analyzing customer signal…"
→ "Searching RocketShip strategy…"
→ "Cross-referencing customer evidence with strategic priorities…"
→ "Evaluating priority and alternatives…"
→ "Drafting recommendation and PRD…"

**Path A · Strategy Available**

Juno returns a grounded priority recommendation with:

P0–P3 or notRecommended recommendation

Customer evidence

Strategic evidence

Traceable citations

Recommendation rationale

Defensible alternative, when appropriate

Confidence indicator

Draft PRD content

The PM reviews and approves the output.

**Path B · Strategy Unavailable**

Juno stops strategic prioritization and displays:

"No strategy source available."

Juno does not generate a generic priority, infer strategic alignment, or substitute its own judgment.

The transcript remains available for PM review, but no priority can be written to the roadmap until the required strategy source is available.

Path C · Evidence Does Not Support Strategic Alignment

If Juno finds customer evidence but cannot verify alignment to the current strategy, it labels the insight:

"Outside current strategy — Unverified"

Juno explains that no supporting strategy clause was found and surfaces the customer evidence for PM review.

The PM may manually override the recommendation, but the unverified recommendation cannot be automatically written to the roadmap.

**AI Moments**

Placement: Inline & Embedded

Juno generates up to three Insight Cards in the Insights column.

Each card contains:

Recommended priority: P0–P3 or notRecommended

Customer evidence: supporting transcript evidence

Strategic alignment: relevant strategic pillar

Strategic traceability: citation to the supporting strategy clause

Rationale: why Juno recommends the priority

Alternative: a defensible alternative when one could improve speed or impact

Confidence: strength of the available evidence

The PRD draft in the third column references the validated Insight Cards and their supporting evidence.

**Value Proposition**

Juno provides augmentation, not autonomous decision-making.

The PM starts with an evidence-backed recommendation rather than a blank page. Juno retrieves, compares, synthesizes, and drafts; the PM validates and decides.

Keeping the Raw Input, Insights, and PRD visible together allows the PM to move directly from:

Customer signal → Evidence → Recommendation → Decision → PRD

without leaving the workflow.

**Fallbacks**

Kill Switch / Manual Override

Every Insight Card includes Manual Override.

The PM can:

Change the recommended P0–P3 priority

Mark the item notRecommended

Edit the rationale

Reject Juno's recommendation

Request regeneration

PRD blocks are individually editable and regenerable.

No roadmap write occurs without PM confirmation.

**Training Signal**

PM overrides are captured as structured feedback.

For example:

Manual demotion → "Strategic-alignment correction"

Repeated similar overrides are flagged for evaluation rather than automatically changing Juno's behavior.

Three or more similar overrides trigger a review signal indicating that retrieval logic, prioritization criteria, or the associated strategic pillar may require clarification.

**Fail-Safe**

If RAG cannot find supporting strategic evidence, Juno does not invent or reconstruct an alignment.

The Insight Card is labeled:

"Outside current strategy — Unverified"

with:

"No supporting strategy clause found."

The PM can review the customer evidence and manually determine the appropriate next step.

Unverified recommendations may be reviewed and edited, but they cannot be written to the roadmap until the verification requirement is satisfied.
